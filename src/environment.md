## Computational Conventions
- Euclidean signature
- Dimensional regularization: d = 4 - epsilon
- FeynCalc dimensional convention: D = 4 - 2 epsilon_FC
- Relation between regulators: epsilon = 2 epsilon_FC

## Notes
- Mathematica is used for symbolic calculations.
- FeynCalc and FeynArts are used for Feynman-diagram generation
  and algebraic manipulations.
