## 1.2 Cutoff and Dimensional Regularization
- **Objective:** Evaluate fundamental tadpole and bubble integrals across two distinct regularization schemes and verify their formal consistency.
- **Methodology:** Parallel evaluation using sharp momentum cutoff and dimensional regularization (DR).

## 1.2.1 Definition of the Tadpole and Bubble Integrals
- **Claim:** Tadpole and bubble integrals are the base building blocks for one-loop corrections.
- **Status:** Established; definitions and the zero-momentum bubble integral $B(0)$ are recorded.

## 1.2.2 Sharp Momentum Cutoff Evaluation
- **Claim:** Tadpole integral $T_\Lambda$ is quadratically divergent; bubble integral $B_\Lambda$ is logarithmically divergent.
- **Status:** Established; integral forms defined and evaluated using sharp momentum cutoff $\Lambda$.

## 1.2.3 Dimensional Regularization Framework
- **Claim:** Integrals are analytically continued to $d=4-\varepsilon$ dimensions using Euler Gamma functions.
- **Status:** Framework established; analytical expressions defined.

## 1.2.4 Laurent Expansions and Pole Structure
- **Claim:** UV divergences correspond to $1/\varepsilon$ poles in DR.
- **Status:** Established; Laurent expansions derived for both tadpole and bubble integrals and compared against cutoff results.

## 1.2.5 Verification of the Derivative Identity
- **Claim:** The zero-momentum bubble integral $B(0)$ satisfies the mass-derivative identity $B(0) = -\partial T / \partial m^2$.
- **Status:** Verified; the identity holds for exact expressions and Laurent expansions, confirming the mathematical consistency of the DR evaluation.

## 1.3 Counterterms and Regulator Independence
- **Objective:** Determine counterterms to cancel ultraviolet divergences and demonstrate that physical scattering amplitudes are regulator-independent.
- **Methodology:** Impose renormalization conditions at zero momentum and compare results between Sharp Cutoff and DR schemes.

## 1.3.1 Renormalization Conditions
- **Claim:** Physical parameters ($m, \lambda$) are uniquely defined by fixed kinematic constraints on the 1PI functions at zero momentum.
- **Status:** Conditions imposed; the system of renormalization equations is fully defined.

## 1.3.2 Derivation of Counterterms
- **Claim:** Renormalization equations uniquely determine the one-loop counterterms $\{\delta m^2, \delta Z_\phi, \delta \lambda\}$.
- **Status:** Counterterms derived; verified that they cancel the divergent pole/cutoff terms consistently across both schemes. Pole coefficients are universal, with $\delta Z_\phi=0$.

## 1.3.3 Regulator Independence and Finite Differences
- **Claim:** Physical momentum-dependent scattering differences (e.g., $B(P^2) - B(0)$) are ultraviolet-finite and identical in both cutoff and DR schemes.
- **Status:** Verified; demonstrated that UV divergences are scheme-dependent while physical observables are regulator-independent.
